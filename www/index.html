Opposites Cancel to Zero

Every dropped number is positive cyan (+N) or negative coral (-N). When two opposite numbers touch (like +3 and -3), they add up to 0, cancel out, and detonate a Zero Blast!

Zero Blast Power Levels

±1 Micro Pop: Basic single-cell clear.
±2 Shockwave: High-impact wave that cracks nearby Dark Matter blocks.
±4 Cross Laser: Fires intense laser beams across the entire row and column!
±8+ Supernova: Obliterates a massive 3×3 area and awards a bonus Swap Token!
+2 + +2 = +4 ✨

Same Numbers Merge & Double

Touching identical numbers combine together and double in value (+2 & +2 = +4, +4 & +4 = +8, -3 & -3 = -6). Merge larger numbers to craft monster Cross Lasers and Supernovas!

Chain Reaction Cascades

When tiles fall into empty spaces after a match, any new matches trigger cascading combos (2x, 3x, 4x...), multiplying your score and quickly recharging your Swap Tokens!

⚡ Quantum Swap Tokens

Press [S] or tap the Swap button to enter Swap Mode. Select any two adjacent tiles to swap their positions on the grid. Use this to ignite trapped combinations or escape containment breaches!

📦 Hold Slot [C]

Don't want to drop the current orb right now? Press [C] or click the Hold slot to store it for later and retrieve your previously held orb.
