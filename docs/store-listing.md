**Full description** (max 4000)

```
Positive meets negative. Everything goes to zero.

Nullfall is a fast, tactile number puzzle where every move can set off a chain reaction. Drag glowing orbs onto the board, pair each number with its opposite, and watch them cancel out in a burst of light. +3 meets −3? ZERO POINT! The bigger the numbers, the bigger the blast.

HOW IT WORKS
• Opposites cancel – drop −5 next to +5 and both vanish in a Zero Blast.
• Same numbers fuse – +2 and +2 merge into +4, then +8, then +16. Grow big numbers on purpose to craft huge explosions.
• Gravity does the rest – cleared space pulls the board down, new neighbours meet, and cascades stack your combo multiplier.
• Keep it under control – Dark Matter rises from below. Blast it apart before the board fills past the danger line.

BIGGER NUMBERS, BIGGER BOOMS
• ±1: a quick pop
• ±2–3: a pulse shockwave that cracks nearby Dark Matter
• ±4–7: cross lasers that sweep a full row and column
• ±8–15: a Supernova that craters a 3×3 area
• ±16 and up: a Hyperspace Singularity that wipes the bottom of the board

POWER UP
• Overdrive – skilful play charges the Overdrive gauge. Fill it to load a super-weapon: Black Hole Vortex, Nova Core or Antimatter Nuke.
• Quantum Swaps – swap any two neighbouring orbs to rescue a bad board or line up the perfect combo.
• Special orbs – Invert (±), Halve (÷2), Double (×2) and the Singularity bomb (0) twist the board in your favour.
• Hold slot – save an orb for later and plan two moves ahead.

THREE WAYS TO PLAY
• Adventure – travel a star map across cosmic sectors, beat hand-built missions and earn up to three stars on each.
• Classic – endless survival. How long can you hold the reactor before it breaches? Chase your best score.
• Challenges – focused tactical missions that test a single skill.

EASY TO LEARN
A hands-on tutorial teaches the basics in minutes, and the Academy explains every mechanic whenever you need a refresher. No timers, no rush – think as long as you like, then go for the big combo.

MADE TO FEEL GOOD
• Satisfying haptics on every blast and merge
• Live synthesized sound that reacts to your combos
• Sleek neon space visuals in a one-handed portrait layout
• Plays offline – no connection needed

Simple addition. Explosive results. Can you reach the Zero Point?
```
